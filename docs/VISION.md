# Vision

## One-line pitch
A Windows 98-style desktop that is Khoumari Adam's portfolio — fun enough that people play with it,
clear enough that a recruiter gets what they need in 30 seconds.

## Who it's for
1. **Recruiters / hiring managers** (embedded, IoT, robotics roles) — need the CV, skills and proof fast.
   The plain `/resume` page and the "Hiring?" card exist for them.
2. **Engineers and makers** — want to see real work: the Breadboard simulator, Lab dashboard, projects.
3. **Anyone who lands on it** — should smile, explore, and share it.

## The problem
Most portfolios are forgettable pages. Adam is an embedded systems engineer (ESP32, robotics,
Raspberry Pi AI home lab) from Bouira, Algeria — he needs to stand out and show hands-on skill.

## Success looks like
- Visitors stay and open several apps; recruiters reach the résumé or contact in ≤ 2 clicks.
- Lighthouse performance stays high; first load stays light (heavy apps lazy-load).
- Works great on phones, in English and Arabic, in both themes.

## Out of scope
- Any backend, database, login, analytics, tracking or cookies.
- Fake content about Adam. Placeholders are fine; invented facts are not.

## Language & locale
English + Arabic (RTL). Fonts: Reem Kufi (Arabic titles), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Arabic text).
